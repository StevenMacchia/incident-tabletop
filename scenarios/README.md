# Scenario library

37 scenarios, 93 versions in total once tailored versions are counted. Each has four timed decisions, the consequences of every option, the lesson and, where relevant, the law.

| Scenario | Written for | Severity | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [The breath-hold challenge](scenarios/challenge.md) | Social media & video, Kids & education | Sev 1 | A dangerous "challenge" is spreading fast on a Friday evening. Contain it without wrecking your weekend team or silencing the people warning against it. |
| [The regulator's request](scenarios/rfi.md) | Social media & video | Sev 2 | An EU regulator asks how your recommender exposes minors to eating-disorder content. You have ten working days, and your data has gaps. |
| [The special-treatment leak](scenarios/vip.md) | Social media & video | Sev 2 | A journalist has screenshots suggesting high-profile accounts get slower, softer enforcement. They want comment within 24 hours. |
| [The counterfeit flood](scenarios/counterfeit.md) | Marketplace & e-commerce | Sev 2 | A luxury brand's lawyers demand that 1,400 listings come down in 48 hours, days before your biggest sales event. |
| [The too-good-to-be-true listings](scenarios/offplatform.md) | Marketplace & e-commerce | Sev 2 | Scammers post cheap consoles and phones, then move buyers off the platform to pay by bank transfer. |
| [The money mule network](scenarios/mules.md) | Fintech & payments | Sev 2 | Young customers are receiving and instantly forwarding stolen money. Many were recruited on social media. |
| [The fake fraud team](scenarios/impersonation.md) | Fintech & payments | Sev 1 | Scammers are calling customers pretending to be your fraud team and persuading them to move savings to a “safe account”. |
| [The gifts that weren't gifts](scenarios/gifting.md) | Gaming, Kids & education | Sev 1 | A parent reports an adult who befriended their 11-year-old in your game with in-game gifts, then moved the chat to another app. |
| [The swatting threat](scenarios/swatting.md) | Gaming, Social media & video | Sev 1 | A popular streamer is hit by a coordinated harassment campaign that escalates to posting their home address. |
| [The date that went wrong](scenarios/assault.md) | Dating | Sev 1 | A user reports being sexually assaulted by a match. The accused is still active and has more dates lined up. |
| [The romance scam ring](scenarios/romance.md) | Dating | Sev 2 | An organized ring is running romance and investment scams through your app with AI-generated profiles. |
| [The election deepfake](scenarios/deepfake.md) | Generative AI | Sev 1 | A realistic fake image of a candidate, apparently made with your model, goes viral days before an election. |
| [The chatbot and the teenager](scenarios/companion.md) | Generative AI, Kids & education | Sev 1 | A parent shares transcripts showing your chatbot roleplaying romance with their 15-year-old and mishandling a mention of self-harm. |
| [The courier at the door](scenarios/courier.md) | Gig, delivery & rentals | Sev 1 | A customer reports being assaulted by a courier. The courier's account turns out to have been rented to someone else. |
| [The hidden camera](scenarios/camera.md) | Gig, delivery & rentals | Sev 2 | A guest finds a hidden camera in a rental bedroom and posts the video. It has millions of views, and the host has 40 other listings. |
| [The image in the class chat](scenarios/classroom.md) | Kids & education | Sev 1 | A student shares an explicit image of a classmate in your app's class chat. Teachers and parents want answers. |
| [The comment section problem](scenarios/comments.md) | Kids & education, Social media & video | Sev 1 | A researcher shows adults leaving predatory, timestamped comments on videos of young children, and recommendations feeding them more. |
| [The account takeover wave](scenarios/ato.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 2 | Attackers use leaked passwords to break into creator accounts with large followings. Their goal: posting crypto scams to millions of followers. |
| [The insider lookup](scenarios/insider.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 2 | A contractor on your review team has been looking up private messages and location history for people who were never in a review queue. |
| [The Saturday night emergency](scenarios/emergency.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 1 | Late on a Saturday, a teenager posts that they will end their life tonight, in a post seen only by a few friends. Police then call asking for data without a warrant. |
| [The bot army](scenarios/bots.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 2 | 40,000 fake accounts sign up overnight to push coordinated political spam. |
| [The vendor goes dark](scenarios/vendor.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 2 | A typhoon takes your moderation vendor's main site offline. 60% of your reviewers are gone for a week and reports of harassment, hate and graphic content are piling up. |
| [The biased-enforcement story](scenarios/bias.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 2 | A journalist's analysis suggests Black creators discussing racism are three times more likely to have posts removed as hate speech. |
| [The recalled toy](scenarios/recall.md) | Marketplace & e-commerce | Sev 1 | A toddler is hospitalized after swallowing magnets from a toy bought on your marketplace. The toy was recalled months ago. |
| [The review factory](scenarios/reviews.md) | Marketplace & e-commerce | Sev 2 | A competitor sends proof that a rival seller is paying for thousands of five-star reviews through a private group. |
| [The sanctions near-miss](scenarios/sanctions.md) | Fintech & payments | Sev 2 | Customers have been sending money to crypto wallets that were just added to a sanctions list, and three transfers are pending. |
| [The coerced customer](scenarios/coerced.md) | Fintech & payments | Sev 2 | A live-in carer appears to be draining an 84-year-old customer's account, and she insists everything is fine. |
| [The recreated attack](scenarios/ugcextremism.md) | Gaming | Sev 1 | A user-built world recreating a real mass shooting is trending among teenage players. |
| [The $3,000 bill](scenarios/lootbox.md) | Gaming | Sev 2 | A 12-year-old spends $3,000 on loot boxes in two weeks, the parent's post goes viral, and a regulator starts asking questions. |
| [The 15-year-old on the app](scenarios/minor.md) | Dating | Sev 1 | A member reports that their match says she's 15. The account has matched with 40 adults. |
| [The location leak](scenarios/location.md) | Dating | Sev 1 | A researcher shows your “distance away” feature can pinpoint members' homes, including in countries where being gay is a crime. |
| [The viral jailbreak](scenarios/jailbreak.md) | Generative AI | Sev 1 | A prompt that tricks your model into giving weapons instructions is spreading on social media. |
| [The cloned voice](scenarios/voiceclone.md) | Generative AI | Sev 1 | Your voice tool was used to clone a teenager's voice for a fake kidnapping call to her parents. |
| [The party house](scenarios/partyhouse.md) | Gig, delivery & rentals | Sev 1 | A house booked for “a quiet family weekend” hosts a 200-person party that ends in a shooting. |
| [The listing that didn't exist](scenarios/fakelisting.md) | Gig, delivery & rentals | Sev 2 | Holiday travelers arrive at rentals that don't exist, after being talked into paying outside your platform. |
| [The takedown order](scenarios/takedown.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 2 | A government orders you to remove posts by journalists criticizing a government minister within 24 hours, citing a vague national security law. |
| [The new law](scenarios/newlaw.md) | Every company type (tailored) | Sev 3 | You launch in a market covered by the UK Online Safety Act's children's safety duties in 90 days, and nobody has started on compliance. |
